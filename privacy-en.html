3. How forms work

When a form is submitted, it sends the operator the visitor's name, phone number, selected website type, consent status and technical submission time. The inquiry is delivered by a Vercel server function and the Resend transactional email service. The form does not request passwords, payment data or documents.

4. Information processed

When the website is opened, the server and technical providers may automatically receive information required to deliver and secure the page: IP address, request date and time, requested page, browser and device type, and technical error logs.

If a visitor contacts the operator by phone, email or MAX, the operator may receive their name, phone number, email address, identifier in the relevant service and message content.

5. Purposes and legal grounds

  • responding to an inquiry and discussing a possible project;
  • preparing a service proposal;
  • operating and securing the website;
  • meeting obligations under the laws of the Russian Federation.

Processing is based on the user's voluntary inquiry and consent, as well as other grounds allowed by Russian law.

6. Processing and retention

Data is processed only to the extent needed to answer an inquiry and continue communication. Messages and inquiries are retained for no more than one year after the latest interaction unless the law requires longer retention. Data may be deleted earlier after consent is withdrawn or following a lawful request, provided no other legal basis for processing remains.

The operator does not sell personal data or use it for automated decision-making.

7. Third-party services

Vercel hosts the website and handles requests; Resend delivers inquiries by email. These providers may process technical information and inquiry content under their own terms. Google Fonts may contact Google servers when fonts load. The MAX button opens an external service governed by its own policies. No web analytics service is currently connected.

8. User rights

You may request information about processing, correction, restriction or deletion of your data, or withdraw consent. Email volodimirnedosekin@yandex.ru and include enough information to identify the relevant inquiry.

9. Information security

The operator takes reasonable organisational and technical measures to limit access and prevent unlawful use, alteration, disclosure or destruction of personal data.

10. Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the website, forms, analytics, hosting or other services change. The current version is always published on this page.
